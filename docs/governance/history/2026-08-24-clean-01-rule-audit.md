This cleanup audit covers the following files at the original audited baseline
`integration/mvp@5f7eccc3447b2e68ccca4bef186c537ffcb0d2a6`:

- `AGENTS.md`
- `docs/governance/OWNER-DEVELOPMENT-CONSTITUTION.md`
- `CONTRIBUTING.md`
- `docs/development-workflow.md`
- `docs/collaboration-workflow.md`
- `docs/branching-strategy.md`
- `docs/module-ownership.md`
- `.github/pull_request_template.md`

`PRESERVE` keeps a rule substantively unchanged. `MODERNIZE` retains its
protection with current, more precise wording. `MERGE` places duplicate rules
under one controlling rule or operational location. `RETIRE` removes an
objectively obsolete, duplicated, or replaced instruction. This completed audit
is historical evidence and does not override the current
[`OWNER-DEVELOPMENT-CONSTITUTION.md`](../OWNER-DEVELOPMENT-CONSTITUTION.md).

## Deletions after consolidation

- `docs/development-workflow.md`: setup, validation, PR, ownership, and
  dependency rules are preserved or modernized in `CONTRIBUTING.md`, the
  Constitution, the PR template, and `docs/module-ownership.md`.
- `docs/collaboration-workflow.md`: branch, review, environment, secret, and
  Owner-control protections are preserved in those same current authorities;
  volatile hosting-plan claims and automatic Ready sequencing are retired.
